Introduction

Bright Secondary School aims to provide vocational training to help students acquire practical skills. These skills can help learners start their own businesses or find employment.

Vocational Courses Offered

1. Tailoring and Fashion Design

Students learn how to make clothes, use sewing machines, repair garments and design different types of clothing.

2. Computer Applications

Students learn basic computer skills such as typing, using Microsoft Word, preparing presentations and using the internet.

3. Agriculture

Students learn about crop growing, animal keeping, soil preparation and modern farming methods.

4. Carpentry and Joinery

Students learn how to make simple furniture and use carpentry tools safely.

5. Catering and Food Production

Students learn food preparation, cooking, hygiene and basic catering skills.

6. Electrical Installation

Students learn basic electrical knowledge and safe electrical practices under proper supervision.

Importance of Vocational Training

  • Helps students gain practical skills.
  • Promotes self-employment.
  • Prepares learners for different careers.
  • Encourages creativity and innovation.
  • Helps students become independent.

Inclusive Vocational Training

The school aims to provide equal training opportunities for all learners, including children with disabilities. Training activities should be adapted according to individual learning needs.

Conclusion

Vocational education is important because it helps students develop useful skills for everyday life and future employment.
