About Bright Secondary School

Bright Secondary School is an educational institution founded by SAVE THE CHILD NGO. The school aims to provide education and practical skills to young people.

The school provides academic education and vocational training. It also aims to support children with disabilities and provide them with opportunities to learn.

Our History

Bright Secondary School was established through the efforts of SAVE THE CHILD NGO to support education and skills development. The school aims to create a good learning environment where students can develop their abilities.

Our Mission

To provide quality education and practical skills that help students become responsible and productive members of society.

Our Vision

To become a school that provides good education, practical skills and equal learning opportunities for all students.

Our Objectives

  • To provide quality secondary education.
  • To develop students' practical skills.
  • To encourage discipline and good behaviour.
  • To promote computer and ICT education.
  • To support learners with disabilities.
  • To prepare students for future careers.
