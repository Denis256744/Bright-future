Introduction

Bright Secondary School provides different facilities to support teaching, learning and the development of students. These facilities are intended to provide a good learning environment.

1. Student Dormitories

The school dormitories provide accommodation for students who stay at school. Students are expected to keep their sleeping areas clean and follow dormitory rules.

2. Computer Laboratory

The computer laboratory helps students learn computer studies and information technology. Students use computers to develop their ICT skills.

3. School Farm

The school farm provides an opportunity for students to learn about agriculture and practical farming activities.

4. Kindergarten

The kindergarten provides a learning environment for young children. It helps children develop basic learning and social skills.

5. Vocational Training Section

The vocational section helps students acquire practical skills that can help them in their future careers.

6. Orphanage for Children with Disabilities

The orphanage provides care and support for children with disabilities. The school aims to promote equal learning opportunities and inclusion.

Importance of School Facilities

These facilities help improve education, practical skills, student welfare and the general development of learners.
