Introduction

School rules help students maintain discipline and good behaviour. Every student at Bright Secondary School is expected to follow the school rules and regulations.

1. General Behaviour

  • Students must respect teachers and other students.
  • Students must obey school rules.
  • Fighting and bullying are not allowed.
  • Students should speak politely to others.
  • Students must keep the school clean.

2. Attendance and Punctuality

  • Students must attend lessons regularly.
  • Students should arrive at school on time.
  • Students must seek permission when absent.
  • Late coming should be avoided.

3. Academic Rules

  • Students must complete their assignments.
  • Students should participate in class activities.
  • Cheating during examinations is not allowed.
  • Students must respect teachers during lessons.

4. School Uniform

  • Students should wear the approved school uniform.
  • Uniforms must be clean and neat.
  • Students should maintain personal hygiene.

5. Care of School Property

  • Students must protect school furniture.
  • Students should not damage school buildings.
  • School equipment must be used properly.

6. Health and Safety

  • Students must follow safety instructions.
  • Dangerous objects are not allowed at school.
  • Students should report accidents to teachers.
  • Students must use laboratory equipment carefully.

7. Dormitory Rules

  • Students should keep dormitories clean.
  • Students must respect other students' property.
  • Students should follow sleeping schedules.

Importance of School Rules

School rules help create a peaceful learning environment. They encourage discipline, responsibility and respect among students.
